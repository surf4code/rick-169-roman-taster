# Repurposing a long video into 3-5 Shorts

Use when: turning a finished or scripted long video into vertical Shorts.

A Short is not a trimmed trailer. It is a self-contained piece that makes sense to
someone who has never seen the long video. Cut moments, not summaries.

## Step 1: find candidate moments

Read the transcript or script and mark moments that:
- make sense alone, with no earlier context needed
- contain one idea only
- have tension, a surprise, a clear answer, or a visible demo
- look good in a tall frame (a face, a close-up, one clear object)
- can be understood with the sound off (captions help)

List 8-10 candidates; keep the best 3-5.

## Step 2: score and pick

| Moment (timestamp) | Standalone | One idea | Payoff | Visual | Total (0-4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

One point per test. Keep moments scoring 3-4. If two Shorts cover the same idea, keep one.

Five angles to spread across the set (one moment per angle when possible):
1. Best claim (the boldest true statement)
2. Best demo (something visible happening)
3. Best mistake (what to avoid)
4. Best story (a mini arc with a turn)
5. Best myth-bust (a belief overturned)

## Step 3: rewrite the hook

The hook of a Short is the first line, and it has seconds, not a paragraph.
- Open on the payoff or the problem, never on "so" or "okay."
- Use short formulas from hooks.md (result first, mistake callout, myth bust, odd fact, stop sign).
- Put a visible line of text on screen in the first moment.
- Score each Short hook with the hooks.md rubric (three options per Short is overkill; two is fine).
- A moment lifted from the long video may need a new first sentence to stand alone.

## Step 4: length

- Cut to the shortest version that still delivers the payoff. Remove setup, filler, and repeated lines.
- Rule of thumb: many creators aim for roughly 20-45 seconds; test your own channel to see what works.
- The platform has a maximum length; as far as I know it allows up to three minutes, but verify the current limit before you plan.
- Do not pad to reach a length target, and do not cut the payoff to hit one.

## Step 5: captions and on-screen text

- Burn in captions; many viewers watch with sound off.
- Short phrases that appear in rhythm with speech; keep to one or two lines.
- Large, high-contrast text with an outline or backing.
- Keep text away from the bottom edge and the right edge, where the app's buttons and caption overlays sit.
- Highlight the one key word per phrase if you highlight at all; do not highlight everything.
- Proofread every caption, especially names, numbers, and terms. Auto-captions make errors.
- If speaking a mix of languages, keep captions in the language the viewer hears and spell consistently.

## Step 6: ending and CTA

- **One CTA per Short.** Choose one: watch the full video, follow, or a link to a related video. Not all three.
- Say it in a few words, after the payoff.
- Option: a loop ending, where the last line connects to the first so replays feel natural. Use only if it fits.
- Never use the Short to tease something the long video does not contain.

## Step 7: metadata for each Short

- Title or caption: plain, keyword-aware, a few words.
- Description: one sentence plus the link to the long video if appropriate.
- Hashtags: zero to three, relevant.
- Keep the Short's promise consistent with the clip.

## Short brief template (one per Short)

```
Short #:            Angle:
Source moment:      (timestamp / script section)
Hook (2 options, scored):
  A: "..."  Score x/5
  B: "..."  Score x/5
Script (spoken, trimmed):
On-screen text:
Visual plan (vertical framing, zoom, cutaways):
Ending line + single CTA:
Title/caption:
Est. length:
Check: makes sense alone? payoff before the end?
```

## Batch plan

| Order | Short | Angle | Post idea |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | Best claim | Day of long video launch |
| 2 | ... | Best demo | A few days after |
| 3 | ... | Best myth | Next |

Posting dates are the user's call; suggest a spread, but do not claim a best time to post.

## Shorts checklist

- [ ] 3-5 Shorts, each from a different moment or angle
- [ ] Each stands alone without the long video
- [ ] Each hook scored; hook lands in the first line
- [ ] Length trimmed to payoff; platform maximum verified
- [ ] Captions burned in, proofread, away from the screen edges
- [ ] Exactly one CTA per Short
- [ ] No claim, number, or source that the long video did not support
