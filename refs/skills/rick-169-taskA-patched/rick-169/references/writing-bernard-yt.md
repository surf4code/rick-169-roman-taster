# Writing, Retention, Akira Host, and Packaging Guide

Synthesizes `bernard-writing` (all narration, rhythm, beat sheet, humor, and monetisation-safe rules, ignoring ONLY its "no clothes" rule) with `youtube-studio` (hooks, retention loops, and titles/thumbnails).

## 1. Bernard Narration DNA

- **Title Formula:** `Why It Sucks to Be [a/the ROLE] (in [PLACE or ERA])`, 50-70 characters.
- **Target Length:** 20-23 minutes (~3,300-3,500 words at 150-165 wpm).
- **Sentence Rhythm:** Average 12-15 words. 15-22% <= 6 words, <12% >= 25 words, ~20% start with `You`.
- **POV and Tense:** 2nd person (`you`), present tense throughout. Epilogue may pull back to omniscient past tense.
- **Opening Line:** Always start with `You're born in [place/era].` Land the first injustice by sentence 2.
- **Dialogue:** Mostly reported speech; reserve short 1-10 word punchlines for comic speech bubbles.
- **Sympathetic Antagonists:** The system (law, class, empire) is the real antagonist; individuals reveal understandable motives.
- **Outfit Override:** Characters wear Ricks-Clips vibrant role-coded outfits so roles read at a glance.

## 2. Monetisation-Safe Wording Table

Apply across script, speech bubbles, labels, titles, thumbnails, description, and tags:

| Avoid Saying | Use Instead (Rotate) |
|---|---|
| sex, intercourse | "they do it", "shares his bed", "does his duty", "consummates the marriage" |
| rape, raped | "forces himself on you", "takes what isn't offered", "violates you" |
| prostitute, whore | "courtesan", "woman of the pleasure quarter", "working woman" |
| brothel | "pleasure house", "house of pleasure" |
| naked, nude | "unclothed", "bare", "stripped of robes" |
| suicide | "takes his own life", "ends her life" |
| strong profanity | period insults ("wretch", "fool", "dog", "curse you") or omit |
| slurs / hate speech | never use, even obscured |

## 3. 10-Beat Architecture (~21 Minutes)

1. `0:00-0:40` Birth Hook (`You're born in...`)
2. `0:40-2:00` Family and System
3. `2:00-4:00` First Turn (plant foreign/period term early)
4. `4:00-9:00` Training, Daily Routine, and Rules
5. `9:00-12:00` Rise / False Hope
6. `12:00-14:00` The Twist (`The truth is...` explains the planted term)
7. `14:00-17:00` The Fall (betrayal + antagonist confession)
8. `17:00-19:30` Crisis (shortest sentences)
9. `19:30-21:00` Climax (plain, zero jokes)
10. `Last 30-60s` Epilogue (verifiable historical fact or irony, no moral lecture)

## 4. Akira Cutaways and Packaging

- **Akira:** 1 cutaway per 2-3 min (`1.5-4.0 s`), flat sage-green `#9CAF88`, 5 expressions (`deadpan`, `smirk`, `shout`, `wide-eyed`, `big_grin`).
- **YouTube Studio:** 3 scored hooks, Open-Loop Ledger (2-3 active loops all paid off), pattern breaks every 20-40 s, 3 title candidates, 3 thumbnail concepts (yellow outlined ALL-CAPS 1-3 words + distressed protagonist + smirking authority + hook prop + landmark).
- **Description:** Exact title, 2-sentence summary, chapters, 8-12 hashtags, mandatory line: `This video is a fictional dramatization inspired by historical accounts of [topic]. While based on real practices, characters and events are exaggerated for storytelling.` Zero affiliate links. Zero closing CTA.
