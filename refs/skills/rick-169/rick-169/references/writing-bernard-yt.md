# Writing, Retention, Akira Host, and Packaging Guide

Synthesizes `bernard-writing` (all narration, rhythm, beat sheet, humor, and monetisation-safe rules, ignoring ONLY its "no clothes" rule) with `youtube-studio` (hooks, retention loops, and titles/thumbnails).

## 1. Bernard Narration DNA

- **Title Formula:** `Why It Sucks to Be [a/the ROLE] (in [PLACE or ERA])`, 50-70 characters. Role reads like a fate. At most one ALL-CAPS emphasis word if needed.
- **Target Length:** 20 to 23 minutes (~3,300 to 3,500 words at 150-165 wpm).
- **Sentence Rhythm:** Avg 12-15 words. 15-22% <= 6 words, <12% >= 25 words, ~20% start with `You`.
- **POV & Tense:** 2nd person (`you`), present tense throughout the life arc. Epilogue may pull back to omniscient past tense.
- **Opening Line:** Always start with `You're born in [place/era].` Land the first injustice or omen by sentence 2.
- **Protagonist Agency:** Mostly acted upon by laws, class, and custom (`Nobody asks what you think.`, `You nod.`, `You say nothing at all.`).
- **Understated Emotion:** Physical silence and action over abstract feeling labels.
- **Dialogue:** Mostly reported speech; reserve short 1-10 word punchlines for direct quotes / speech bubbles.
- **Sympathetic Antagonists:** The system is the true villain; human antagonists reveal understandable reasons later.

## 2. Connective Tissue (Vary Naturally)

- Time jumps (every 2-3 min): `Years pass as you turn N.`, `You've just turned N when...`, `You're N when everything starts to change.`
- Reactions/pivots: `Immediately,` (~5/script), `Still,`, `Instead,`, `Turns out...`, `After all,`.
- World explanations: `The truth is,`, `The thing is,`, `To be honest,` (3-6 sentences inside a scene).
- Turns (max 2-3): `But life has other plans.`, `But life is never fair.`
- Dramatic irony (1-2): `What you don't know is...`, `What you don't realize is...`
- Cap `"as"` simultaneity clauses to <= 1 per 80-100 words.

## 3. Dry Humor & Dark Subject Matter

- Gloss-the-translation, modern commerce metaphors, deadpan understatement, rule-of-three with a petty third item, observant servants/children.
- Wry Act 1 -> warmer/hopeful midpoint -> humor thins in Act 3 -> zero jokes at the moment of death/climax.
- Write dark historical realities honestly in the same calm voice without moralizing or apologizing.

## 4. World-Building Checklist

- Plant 1 foreign/period term early and unexplained; explain mid-story; pay off at climax.
- Include 3-5 authentic terms/titles, 1 trapping law/rule, 1 concrete money/price fact, 1 sensory detail per location, 1 daily-routine montage, 1 recurring physical motif prop, and 1 real verifiable historical fact for the epilogue.
- **Override:** Characters wear Ricks-Clips vibrant role-coded outfits/accents (ignoring Bernard's "no clothes" rule).

## 5. Monetisation-Safe Wording (Active by Default)

Keep every event intact; substitute explicit sexual/profane terms across script, bubbles, labels, titles, thumbnails, description, and tags:

| Avoid Saying | Use Instead (Rotate) |
|---|---|
| sex, intercourse | "they do it", "shares his bed", "does his duty", "consummates the marriage" |
| rape, raped | "forces himself on you", "takes what isn't offered", "violates you", "has his way with you" |
| prostitute, whore, slut | "courtesan", "woman of the pleasure quarter", "working woman" |
| brothel | "pleasure house", "house of pleasure" |
| naked, nude, strips | "unclothed", "bare", "stripped of robes" |
| genitals, breasts, arousal | "body", "chest", "pleasure" (or omit) |
| suicide | "takes his own life", "ends her life" |
| strong profanity | period insults ("wretch", "fool", "dog", "curse you") or omit |
| slurs / hate speech | never use, even obscured |

- **Minors:** Zero sexual wording or imagery for anyone under 18; skip time with an age card.

## 6. 10-Beat Architecture (~21 Minutes)

| Timecode | Beat | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:40 | Birth Hook | `You're born in...` + era/place + first trap |
| 0:40-2:00 | Family & System | Status, household rules, early dry jokes |
| 2:00-4:00 | First Turn | Role locked in; plant foreign term |
| 4:00-9:00 | Training & Rules | Daily routine, hazards, ally, time jump |
| 9:00-12:00 | Rise / False Hope | Comfort, privilege, or hope of freedom |
| 12:00-14:00 | The Twist | Hidden truth breaks comfort (`The truth is...`) |
| 14:00-17:00 | The Fall | Betrayal, trap closes, antagonist confession |
| 17:00-19:30 | Crisis | Impossible choice; shortest sentences |
| 19:30-21:00 | Climax | Plain, calm delivery; zero jokes |
| Last 30-60s | Epilogue | Pull back; real historical fact or irony; no lecture |

## 7. Akira Host Cutaways

- 1 cutaway every 2-3 minutes (`1.5-4.0 s`), centered on flat sage-green `#9CAF88` backdrop.
- Fixed 5 expressions: `deadpan`, `smirk`, `shout`, `wide-eyed`, `big_grin`.
- Ask once for the user's Akira reference image; use placeholder until provided.

## 8. YouTube Studio Hooks, Retention & Packaging

- **Hooks:** Draft 3 scored hook options (Cold Open, Fate-First, Contrast) honoring `You're born in...` and matching title/thumbnail promise.
- **Open-Loop Ledger:** Track 2-3 active loops (`Opened at` -> `Paid off at`). Every loop must pay off honestly.
- **Pattern Breaks & Re-Hooks:** Visual/audio/verbal break every 20-40 s; verbal re-hook before Acts 2 & 3.
- **Titles:** 3 options (50-65 chars, Bernard pattern) + recommendation.
- **Thumbnails:** 3 concepts: 1-3 bold yellow ALL-CAPS black-outlined words (not repeating title), big distressed protagonist face, smirking authority, 1 hook prop, 1 cultural landmark; monetisation-safe (no gore/nooses).
- **Description (Zero Affiliates, Zero Closing CTA):** Exact title, 2-sentence video-specific summary, beat chapters, 8-12 accurate hashtags, mandatory disclaimer (`This video is a fictional dramatization inspired by historical accounts of [topic]. While based on real practices, characters and events are exaggerated for storytelling.`), and 25-30 accurate tags.
